STEP00 pathway

1 · Explore

Begin with a real place

Search, navigate, or choose a location and view its surrounding geographic context.

2 · Compare

Look through more than one perspective

Compare observations, reference imagery, and map-producer interpretations. Check source, date or period, role, and spatial support where available.

3 · Establish

Confirm the geographic anchor

Document the location that anchors the investigation and how it was selected.

4 · Validate

Reveal the nested geography

Inspect the AOI, PSU, and SSU supports, then run the geometry audit and review unresolved status.

5 · Preserve

Create the baseline archive

Carry forward the geography, evidence roles, retrieval history, and initial place question without hiding missing or unresolved evidence.

Pathway 03

Continue through reSearch

A Traveling Archive can be checked out for review and extension. Incomplete labels, zero-result searches, failed retrievals, and unresolved evidence continue downstream as explicit open work. Accepted changes become non-overwriting descendants with parent lineage rather than silent replacements.

Public Visitor Center: discovery and readable artifacts. Traveling Archive: portable evidence, provenance, relationships, and open work. Canonical AaP3Km repository: architecture and accepted-record control.
