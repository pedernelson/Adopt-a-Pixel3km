Pathway 02

Learn through recurring ground-to-satellite evidence

Reusable learning entry point: course, workshop, cohort, and independent-study implementations can link here without embedding a course number in the public architecture.

The course pathway begins with a GLOBE Observer record at an actual measurement location and time. Students inspect directional ground photographs, search the satellite and remote-sensing registry for valid measurement partners, record temporal and spatial correspondence, and accumulate weekly evidence for a final adjacent-view table.

Ground-to-satellite evidence workflow

1 · Ground anchor

Verify the observation

Use the GLOBE measurement coordinates, observation date and time, location accuracy when supplied, directional photographs, and stable observation identity.

2 · Partner search

Search the sensor registry

Record every retrieval attempt and distinguish not attempted, zero results, failed retrieval, QA rejection, discovery-only access, and accepted evidence.

3 · Correspondence

Evaluate the relationship

Do not assume same place, same time, or equivalent spatial support. Preserve acquisition time, product type, footprint or pixel support, bands, QA, and processing lineage.

4 · Weekly record

Accept evidence without hiding gaps

Save one or more defensible measurement partners beside the ground photographs. Keep map-producer interpretations separate from source observations.

5 · Term synthesis

Build the adjacent-view table

Compare recurring places, temporal coverage, measurement differences, spatial patterns, explanatory hypotheses, and unresolved uncertainty across the term.

Pathway 03

Continue through reSearch

A Traveling Archive can be checked out for review and extension. Incomplete labels, zero-result searches, failed retrievals, and unresolved evidence continue downstream as explicit open work. Accepted changes become non-overwriting descendants with parent lineage rather than silent replacements.

Public Visitor Center: discovery and readable artifacts. Traveling Archive: portable evidence, provenance, relationships, and open work. Canonical AaP3Km repository: architecture and accepted-record control.
