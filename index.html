Place Biography Framework

Five synchronized evidence timelines

Earth System

Physical history, climate, disturbance, hydrology, vegetation, and recovery.

Human Intent

Plans, proposals, policy, restoration, management, and decisions.

Human Experience

Community observations, memories, cultural relationships, and lived experience.

Observations

Survey records, field photos, GLOBE Observer, aerial imagery, Landsat, Sentinel, and other sensors.

Interpretation History

Labels, maps, analyses, uncertainty, revision, reconciliation, and accepted archive states.

Archive candidate pipeline

Group by AOI centerpoint, preserve measurement coordinates

The AOI centerpoint identifies the Traveling Archive. Each SSU and GLOBE record retains its actual measurement or interpretation coordinate, support, date, source, quality state, and provenance.

1. Resolve place identity

Derive or verify the AOI centerpoint, assign the durable MGRS Place Code, identify country and region, and record coordinate roles.

2. Assemble evidence

Attach SSU labels, PSU relationships, GLOBE measurement coordinates and photos, WorldCover results, dates, source files, and unresolved records without collapsing native identities.

3. Review before featuring

Apply coordinate sanity checks, deduplication, label harmonization, disclosure and license review, evidence-status flags, and archive-readiness status.

Public-display rule: raw cohort exports contain contributor identifiers. Public cards should minimize personal information while controlled evidence records preserve necessary scientific provenance.
