Place Biography Framework

Five synchronized evidence timelines

Earth System

Physical history, climate, disturbance, hydrology, vegetation, and recovery.

Human Intent

Plans, proposals, policy, restoration, management, and decisions.

Human Experience

Community observations, memories, cultural relationships, and lived experience.

Observations

Survey records, field photographs, GLOBE Observer, aerial imagery, Landsat, Sentinel, and other sensors.

Interpretation History

Labels, maps, publications, analyses, uncertainty, revision, reconciliation, and accepted archive states.

Project-wide style standard

One vocabulary across maps, measurements, repositories, and archives

Names

  • Adopt-a-Pixel 3 km (AaP3Km) on first use
  • Area of Interest (AOI), Primary Sample Unit (PSU), Secondary Sample Unit (SSU)
  • PSU 0 in prose; PSU-00 in fixed-width labels and identifiers

Measurements

  • 5 m, 100 m, 3 km
  • 5 m × 5 m, 100 m × 100 m, 3 km × 3 km
  • Coordinates display as latitude, longitude with coordinate role

Repository roles

AaP3Km is the canonical architecture and accepted-record repository. Adopt-a-Pixel3km is the public presentation and discovery repository.

Publication and archives are checkpoints, not endpoints
