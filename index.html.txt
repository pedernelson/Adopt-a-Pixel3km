<section id="grids"><p class="eyebrow">Two grid systems, one physical place</p><h2>Why compare the AaP3Km and HLS grids?</h2><p>Both systems help people return to Earth observations through space and time, but they begin from different design needs. At the Welcome Center level, the map shows HLS product tiles together with the 3 km × 3 km AaP3Km place areas. Open STEP00 or a Traveling Archive when you are ready to examine finer sampling and observation details.</p><div class="grid-compare"><article class="grid-system aap"><span class="status">Place-sampling framework</span><h3>Adopt-a-Pixel 3 km</h3><p>A person selects a physical location with a map poke or another documented anchor. That location becomes the center of a 3 km × 3 km Area of Interest and PSU-00. The surrounding sampling grid is then constructed from that anchor.</p><div class="ladder"><div class="rung"><strong>Physical place</strong>Exists on Earth before any coordinate or grid is assigned.</div><div class="rung"><strong>Map poke and documented anchor</strong>Records the digital reference used to begin the investigation.</div><div class="rung"><strong>3 km × 3 km AOI</strong>Provides the larger place context.</div><div class="rung"><strong>37 PSUs</strong>PSU-00 at the anchor plus PSU-01 through PSU-36 in the surrounding design.</div><div class="rung"><strong>3,700 SSUs</strong>Interpretation locations with explicit 5 m × 5 m footprints.</div></div></article><div class="grid-bridge" aria-hidden="true">↔</div><article class="grid-system hls"><span class="status">Measurement-product framework</span><h3>Harmonized Landsat and Sentinel-2</h3><p>HLS places Landsat and Sentinel-2 measurements into the same modified MGRS/UTM tiling system and a common 30 m measurement grid so observations can be stacked and compared through time.</p><div class="ladder"><div class="rung"><strong>Global MGRS/UTM framework</strong>Organizes product geography around the world.</div><div class="rung"><strong>HLS product tile</strong>Approximately 110 km across and identified by an MGRS-aligned tile code.</div><div class="rung"><strong>30 m measurement cells</strong>Common cells used by the harmonized L30 and S30 products.</div><div class="rung"><strong>Repeated acquisitions</strong>Measurements from different dates can be stacked at the same grid location.</div></div></article></div><div class="student-note"><strong>They are related, but neither grid is a subdivision of the other.</strong> HLS organizes delivered satellite measurements. AaP3Km organizes a persistent place, its sampling supports, observations, interpretations, and future revisits. On the map, the two systems meet through spatial intersection and support comparison.</div><div class="question"><strong>Question to carry into the map:</strong> Which HLS product tile contains or overlaps each 3 km × 3 km AaP3Km place area, and what does each framework help you understand about the same physical place?</div><p class="mini">The Welcome Center is an orientation map. It shows HLS product-tile context and unfilled 3 km × 3 km AaP3Km areas.
