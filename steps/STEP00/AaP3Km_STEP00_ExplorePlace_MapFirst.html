STEP00 purpose

Create a Traveling Archive for this place

STEP00 establishes the location, builds its AOI/PSU/SSU geography, gathers available evidence, and saves the result as a portable Traveling Archive. Saving the archive is the goal, even when review items or unresolved work remain. Only missing geography or an actual archive-generation/integrity failure can stop creation.
